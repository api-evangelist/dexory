# Dexory

> Dexory is a UK-based warehouse intelligence and robotics company whose DexoryView platform pairs autonomous mobile scanning robots with physical AI to deliver real-time "ground truth" visibility across warehouses and distribution centers. Its robots scan over 10,000 pallet locations per hour up to 18m of racking height, building a live digital twin that drives inventory accuracy, space optimization, and operational analytics.

This is an API Evangelist network company profile. Dexory does not publish a public developer portal or OpenAPI at this time; the DexoryView platform exposes data through pre-built connectors and integrations into warehouse/ERP systems (SAP EWM, Manhattan, Blue Yonder, Körber) rather than a documented public API.
